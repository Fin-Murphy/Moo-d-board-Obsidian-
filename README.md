## Description 
This is a super simple Obsidian plugin that allows you to specify folders on your machine (regardless of whether or not they are in your repo) and pull their content into a clean and dynamic pinterest-style mood board layout.
Simply configure which folders you want to display in your mood board, and open the board anywhere from either the sidebar or the command palette. Refresh and Reshuffle photos at whim from inside Obsidian.

Yes I drew the cow myself. No slop art here. 

## Installation

Clone and drop into your ```.obsidian/plugins``` folder inside your vault. If it doesn't load right away, edit your ```.obsidian/community-plugins.json```.
